The Tday Tasks widget shows your tasks from a Tday integration for a chosen view — today's tasks, scheduled (non‑overdue) tasks, overdue tasks, or floater (undated) tasks. You can complete, add, edit and delete tasks directly from Homarr.

### Interactions

- Tick the **checkbox** to mark a task complete; an **Undo** toast lets you reverse it.
- Click **+ Add task** to create one or more tasks (one per line), choosing a priority, list and due date.
- Hover a task and click the **pencil** to edit its title, priority, list and due date.
- While editing, click **Delete** (then confirm) to remove the task.
